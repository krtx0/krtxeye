About Dr. Siddhesh Bapat

Dr. Siddhesh Bapat is a highly regarded Ophthalmologist practicing at Allcure Superspeciality Hospital, Jogeshwari East, Mumbai. Known for his patient-first philosophy, Dr. Bapat takes the time to understand each patient's concerns and explains every step of their treatment clearly.

His expertise spans cataract surgery, retina care, LASIK, and complex cases — with an approach that has earned the trust of hundreds of families across Mumbai. Dr. Bapat believes in treating each patient as family, ensuring they feel heard, understood, and cared for throughout their eye health journey.
